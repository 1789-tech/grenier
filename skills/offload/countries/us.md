# Offload — United States (us)

Currency: $ · Local pickup is common and free; shipping for smaller/valuable items.

## Sell — individually

| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Furniture, appliances, decor, bikes, tools, baby gear | The default for most local items. Huge reach, free, local pickup. |
| **Craigslist** | Furniture, appliances, "curb alert" free items, local pickup | No account friction; cash + pickup. Meet in public for safety. |
| **eBay** | Electronics, collectibles, shippable/niche items, national buyers | Fees apply; great for anything mailable with real value. |
| **Poshmark / Depop** | Clothes, shoes, accessories, fashion | Poshmark = mainstream resale; Depop = younger/vintage. |
| **OfferUp** | Local goods, similar to Marketplace | Strong in many metros. |
| **Specialized** | Trade-in for phones/tech (Gazelle, Decluttr, carrier trade-in), sneakers (StockX, GOAT) | Best price for the right category. |

## Sell — in bulk (volume, minimal effort)

- **Books / CDs / DVDs / games** → **Decluttr / Ziffit**: scan barcodes, they
  quote and pay shipping. Low per-item, zero friction.
- **Clothes in bulk** → thredUP "Clean Out" kit (mail a bag; low payout but
  effortless), or a yard sale.
- **Everything at once** → yard/garage sale to clear low-value items in a day.

## Donate

- **Goodwill / Salvation Army** — clothes, housewares, furniture, books. Some
  offer donation pickup for large items. Tax receipt available.
- **Habitat for Humanity ReStore** — furniture, building materials, appliances;
  will often pick up.
- **Buy Nothing groups** (Facebook / app) — hyperlocal give-away; a neighbor
  picks it up today. Great for good-but-cheap items.
- **Freecycle** — email-list give-away network.
- **Local charities / shelters** — for specific items (coats, kitchenware, etc.).

## Recycle / dispose (correctly)

- **E-waste** — Best Buy in-store recycling, Staples, or municipal e-waste events.
  Never in household trash.
- **Batteries, bulbs, paint** — Home Depot / Lowe's drop-offs (batteries, bulbs);
  PaintCare sites for paint; Call2Recycle for batteries.
- **Bulky items / furniture (dead)** — municipal bulk pickup (schedule with your
  city/hauler) or the transfer station/landfill.
- **Textiles (worn/damaged)** — many charities take rag-grade textiles for
  recycling; check for retailer take-back programs.
- **Household hazardous waste** (solvents, chemicals) — county HHW drop-off.
  Never down the drain or in the trash.
