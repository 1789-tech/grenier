# grenier — France (fr)

Currency: € · Language: French (write listings and replies in French when the
user writes in French). Default to local pickup ("remise en main propre") where
possible — no fees, fast.

France bias in one line: **Leboncoin for local value**, **Vinted for fashion &
small shippable goods and draft-heavy workflows**, **Momox / RecycLivre / Gibert
for books/media bulk**, **Emmaüs / Geev / ressourceries for
low-value-but-useful goods**, **déchèterie / DEEE for broken or unsafe items**.

Default balanced cutoff: **20 € / 20 min / 20 days**. Loosen for max cash,
tighten hard for gone fast.

## Channels — where to sell / donate / recycle (for `disposition.md`)

### Sell — individually

| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Leboncoin** | Furniture, appliances, decor, tech, bikes, tools, baby gear | The default for most items > 15 €. Local pickup or shipping. |
| **Vinted** | Clothes, shoes, bags, accessories; increasingly home/books/games | Buyer pays fees, easy shipping. Draft-first is high value when filling many items. |
| **eBay** | Collectibles, rare parts, niche tech, buyers outside France | Wider reach, shipping. |
| **Vide-greniers / brocante** | High volume of small, cheap items at once | Clear everything in one day without listing each. |
| **Specialized** | Refurb tech (Back Market), instruments (Zikinf), luxury/watches (Vestiaire Collective), design furniture (Selency) | Niche audiences, better prices for the right item. |

### Sell — in bulk (volume, minimal effort)

- **Books** → **Momox / RecycLivre / Gibert (buy-back)**: scan the ISBNs, they buy
  and pay shipping. Low per-item price, zero friction; RecycLivre is
  solidarity-focused.
- **CDs / DVDs / games / consoles** → Momox, Easy Cash, Cash Converters.
- **Clothes in bulk** → Vinted "dressing", or donation (below), or textile
  buy-back bins (Patatam).

### Donate

- **Emmaüs** — furniture, appliances, dishes, clothes, books. Some branches
  collect large volumes at home. The reflex for emptying fast and usefully.
- **Geev** — peer-to-peer giving app: post a photo, someone comes to collect.
  Unbeatable for moving low-market-value-but-still-useful things fast.
- **Donnons.org** — peer-to-peer giving, no app.
- **Local associations** — Croix-Rouge, Secours Populaire, Restos du Cœur (by
  item), neighborhood ressourceries / recycleries.

### Recycle / dispose (correctly)

- **Déchèterie** — rubble, bulky waste, wood, metals, garden waste.
- **Appliances & electronics (DEEE)** — taken back in-store on purchase (1-for-1)
  or at the déchèterie; ecosystem drop-off points. Never in the trash.
- **Batteries, bulbs, cartridges** — bins in supermarkets / DIY stores.
- **Worn/damaged textiles** (not donatable) — Le Relais / Refashion bins:
  recycled into rags/insulation even with holes.
- **Medicines** — pharmacy (Cyclamed). **Paints/solvents/chemicals** — déchèterie
  hazardous-waste section. Never down the drain or in the trash.
- **Bulky items ("encombrants")** — municipal collection by appointment in many
  towns.

## Pricing — realistic value & the one move (for `pricing.md`)

### Priority mapping

| Priority | Default move | Pricing stance |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| **max cash** | Best-fit resale channel, often item-by-item | Price near the realistic high end; accept slower sale and negotiation. |
| **balanced** | One clean listing or one bulk route | Price in the middle-low of the range so it moves in days. |
| **gone fast** | Pickup, buy-back, donation, Geev, recycling | Price low or skip sale if hassle beats money. |

### France channel heuristics

Fee/terms claims are time-sensitive. Treat these as last verified on
**2026-07-06** and re-check the linked platform help before merge or when exact
fees matter.

- Vinted: current public help describes selling as free for the seller; buyers
  pay buyer protection and shipping. Do not overprice 3-5 € items because buyer
  all-in cost rises quickly. Sources: vinted.fr/how_it_works,
  vinted.fr/help/26-quelles-sont-les-bases-de-la-vente,
  vinted.fr/help/25-principes-de-base-pour-les-achats.
- Leboncoin: strong default for local handoff, but category rules and listing
  limits matter; do not turn 25 small objects into 25 separate ads. Source:
  leboncoin.fr/dc/rules.
- Vestiaire Collective: only for authenticable luxury/designer goods; seller fees
  are material enough that vague premium bags should not go there. Source:
  faq.vestiairecollective.com seller selling fees.
- Emmaüs / ecosystem / Refashion: trusted exit routes for useful goods,
  electronics and textiles when sale value is low or trust risk is high. Sources:
  emmaus-france.org/donner, ecosystem.eco/donner-recycler, refashion.fr.

| Item type | Best default | When to avoid |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Furniture, appliances, bikes, tools, baby gear | **Leboncoin**, local pickup | Avoid if value < 20 € balanced or transport/messages will be painful. |
| Clothes, shoes, bags, accessories | **Vinted**, preferably bundles by size/style | Avoid single low-value basics unless bundled. |
| Books | **Momox / RecycLivre / Gibert** for ordinary lots; **Leboncoin/eBay** for valuable art/rare books | Avoid listing ordinary paperbacks one by one. |
| CDs/DVDs/games/consoles | **Momox / Easy Cash / Cash Converters** for fast cash; **Leboncoin/eBay** for valuable items | Avoid low-value singles. |
| Decor, small household goods | **Leboncoin** only if visually attractive or bundled; otherwise **Geev/Emmaüs** | Avoid standalone listings under 15 €. |
| Broken electronics/appliances | **DEEE / déchèterie / store take-back** | Do not sell as working; "pour pièces" only for niche items. |

### Price heuristics

- **Leboncoin**: haggling is normal. Max cash → list 10-15% above target. Balanced
  → list at target. Gone fast → list 15-30% under the middle of the range or
  donate.
- **Vinted**: bundle low-value clothes; buyers send offers. A lot priced fairly
  beats ten 3 € decisions.
- **Books/media buy-back**: expect low per-item return (cents to a few euros) but
  it removes nearly all listing work. For ordinary paperbacks, usually the correct
  mental-load trade.
- **Dépôt-vente / consignment**: only for higher-value furniture, design pieces,
  luxury, or low-hassle-but-not-"gone-today" cases. Mention commission/handoff
  friction briefly.
- **Donation/give-away threshold**: under roughly **20 € net** in balanced mode, or when the
  item needs cleaning, transport, dimensions, many messages, or awkward pickup,
  recommend donation/Geev/recycling unless it's part of a bundle.

### Bundling rules

- Books: split into **ordinary bulk** (buy-back/donate) and **valuable few** (sell
  individually).
- Baby/kids clothes: bundle by **size + season**.
- Adult basics: bundle by **size + style**, or donate if the brands are weak.
- Kitchenware/decor: bundle as "lot premier appartement" or "lot cuisine".
- Toys: bundle by age/license only if complete and clean; otherwise donate.

## Listing — how to draft the ad (for `listing.md`)

### Title format

- Pattern: **Marque + Modèle + info clé + état**.
  e.g. `Vélo de ville Btwin Elops 520 - Taille M - Bon état`.
- Include searchable keywords buyers actually type. No emojis, no ALL CAPS.

### Pricing norms

- Haggling is expected on **Leboncoin**. Start ~10–15% above your target so
  there's room to come down.
- On **Vinted**, buyers use "offres" — price a touch high, accept a reasonable
  counter.
- Round to clean numbers (25 €, 40 €, not 37 €).

### Categories (by platform)

- **Leboncoin** — pick the closest native category (Mobilier, Électroménager,
  Vélos, Informatique, Vêtements, Puériculture…). Fill "état" and location.
- **Vinted** — category + brand + size + condition are mandatory-ish; fill them,
  they drive discovery.

### What buyers expect

- **Remise en main propre** by default (no fees, meet locally) or shipping via
  Mondial Relay / Colissimo. State which.
- Honest condition with visible defects mentioned — French buyers ping fast on
  hidden flaws.
- Dimensions for furniture, size for clothes, working status for electronics.
