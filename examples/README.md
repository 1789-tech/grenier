# grenier examples

Concrete cases, from clutter to a published listing. Each shows **what you say**
to Claude (once the one `grenier` Skill is installed) and **what you get** — no
command to remember, no sub-skill to pick, you describe your situation in plain
language and grenier walks into the right stage.
