# Example — drafting a bike ad

**Stage:** listing · one Skill: `grenier`

## What you say

> "I want to sell my city bike. A Trek FX 2, blue, size M, 3 years old, good
> condition, tires a bit worn."

(or: you paste a photo and Claude reads the brand/model/visible condition.)

## What Claude hands you back (abbreviated)

*(US recipe — reads `countries/us.md`, prices in $)*

```
TITLE: Trek FX 2 Hybrid Bike - Size M - Good Condition
PRICE: $220 (range: $170–$280) OBO
PLATFORM: Facebook Marketplace — local, free, big reach
CATEGORY: Bicycles > Hybrid

Trek FX 2 hybrid, size M (fits ~5'5"–6'0"), blue. Bought 3 years ago,
lightly ridden, fully working: brakes, gears and lights all good. Tires a
bit worn (plan a swap eventually, ~$30). Kickstand included.
Selling due to a move. Local pickup only, cash.

Handover: porch pickup, Austin 78704
```

**Photo tips:** shoot in daylight, whole bike in profile on a neutral background
+ a close-up of the worn tires (show the defect = honest ad = sells faster).

**Assumptions:** I assumed an average used price for this model and "good"
condition — adjust the price if yours is better/worse. Prices are illustrative.
