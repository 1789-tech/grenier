# Example — UK user with electronics and e-waste

**Skill:** `sell` *(UK recipe — recycling routes matter here)*

## What you say

> "Manchester here. I've got an old but working laptop, a dead microwave, a box
> of tangled cables and chargers, and a couple of old phones. What do I do?"

## What Claude does (abbreviated)

`sell` reads the country (UK) → opens `countries/uk.md` and splits sellable from
e-waste:

- **Working laptop** → *eBay UK* (postable, real value) or *Facebook
  Marketplace* for collection. Wipe it first. → `listing` for the ad.
- **Old phones** → *musicMagpie / CeX* trade-in — instant quote, they handle
  postage. Zero friction for a fair price.
- **Dead microwave** → *WEEE*: retailer take-back on a new purchase, or the
  Household Waste Recycling Centre. **Never general waste.**
- **Cables & chargers** → small-electricals recycling at the HWRC or a
  supermarket electricals bank. Working ones → *Freegle* give-away.

Rule applied: sell what has value and sells fast; recycle the dead stuff
**properly** — WEEE, not the bin.

> A US user with the same pile would go Best Buy e-waste / Decluttr / Facebook
> Marketplace instead — from `countries/us.md`. Prices are illustrative.
